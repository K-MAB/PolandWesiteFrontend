# Store Business Rules

- Vistula Components is the only seller and store operator.
- Customers cannot open shops, list products or sell to other visitors.
- Products have one public base price plus optional public promotion and volume tiers.
- The storefront displays one final tax-free amount and currency.
- Adding an item does not reserve stock; the backend reserves it during checkout.
- Quantity must satisfy MOQ and increment rules.
- A customer may read and change only their own profile, cart, addresses and orders.
- Checkout collects delivery details and sends payment to a hosted provider.
- Admin users manage the shared catalog, customers, orders, prices, inventory and content.
