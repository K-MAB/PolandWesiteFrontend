# Roadmap

## Completed frontend

Storefront, catalog, categories, product pages, comparison, cart, checkout, customer account, single-store administration, product import and three locales.

## Backend phase

Authentication, customer persistence, cart synchronization, stock reservation, hosted payment sessions, order creation, fulfillment and transactional email.

## Production hardening

Accessibility audit, performance budgets, security review, observability, backup recovery and load testing.
