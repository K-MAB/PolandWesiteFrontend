# Features

## Storefront

- responsive homepage with categories and product sections
- search, manufacturer/category filters, sorting and pagination
- 50 localized product pages with stock, specifications and volume prices
- product comparison
- persistent cart, quantity controls and checkout
- one visible final price without a tax field

## Customer account

- demo sign-in and simple registration
- account overview, favorites and delivery addresses
- private order list and order detail

## Single-store administration

- sales dashboard and order trend
- customer list and account status
- product, category and manufacturer management
- order status, public price campaigns and inventory adjustment
- CSV/XLSX product import, content management, staff, audit and settings

The frontend uses mock repositories. Real authentication, persistence, payment capture and fulfillment APIs remain backend work.
